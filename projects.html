Research beyond the thesis.

NEOM Nature Reserve Challenge — ESGI 195

University of Exeter · July 2026

Interdisciplinary work on estimating and forecasting vegetation carbon capture using Sentinel-2 satellite imagery, drone data and environmental indicators. Contributions included spatial modelling, data fusion, statistical calibration and uncertainty quantification.

Data-driven epidemiological dependence learning

AIMS Research and Innovation Centre, Kigali · August 2026

Collaborative research on learned dependence structures for malaria surveillance and risk modelling within an endemic–epidemic framework. The work supports an ongoing manuscript on epidemiological connectivity.

Hourly particulate matter modelling

Bavaria, Germany

Development of LASSO-based estimation of spatial and temporal autoregressive effects for high-frequency PM10 concentrations, with predictive validation against alternative dependence specifications.

UK equity volatility networks

Financial dependence modelling

Learning directed volatility-dependence structures among UK equities using penalised spatiotemporal ARCH methodology and comparing learned structures with predefined sector, distance, correlation and autoregressive-similarity alternatives.
