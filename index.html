About

Data-driven statistical dependence

My research focuses on situations where the dependence structure among observations, locations or units is unknown or difficult to specify a priori. I develop sparse and regularised estimation methods that allow these structures to be learned directly from data.

My doctoral work brings together spatial statistics, time-series modelling, statistical learning, numerical optimisation and computational statistics.
