Teaching approach

Learning Through Application

My teaching emphasises connecting statistical theory with practical examples, encouraging students to understand both the mathematical reasoning behind methods and their appropriate use in data analysis.

I aim to make technical material accessible through structured explanations, worked examples and opportunities for independent problem-solving.
